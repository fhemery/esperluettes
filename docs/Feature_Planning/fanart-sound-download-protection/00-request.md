# Discourage downloading of fanart sounds — request

*Written by the user. Free form, may be three lines. Everything below is
optional prompting, not a form to fill.*

## What I want

Prevent sounds from being downloaded. The same way we prevented text from being
copy-pasted, limit the ability of people to download the raw mp3 fanart files.
Preventing right click would be one step. If we can do more to prevent saving
and sharing links etc., that would be appreciated.

## Why

Protect the authors of sound fanarts: the raw mp3 files should not be trivially
downloadable or shareable.

## Constraints or ideas I already have

- Same spirit as the existing text copy-paste protection.
- Disabling right click is one step; go further where feasible (saving, sharing links, etc.).
