# Notify Discord when audio fanart is published — request

*Written by the user. Free form, may be three lines. Everything below is
optional prompting, not a form to fill.*

## What I want

Send the audio fanart to Discord as well as the fanart images. The goal is not
to make the sounds available on Discord, but to have a notification that they
are published, so we can link to the chapter and the book.

The notification should tell us:
- who contributed (slug, name, avatar I guess)
- on which chapter (id, slug)
- on which story (id, slug)

## Why

Image fanart already triggers a Discord notification; audio fanart does not, so
publications of sounds go unnoticed.

## Constraints or ideas I already have

- The sound file itself does not need to be available on Discord — notification
  only, with links to the chapter and the story.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Making the audio playable/downloadable on Discord.
