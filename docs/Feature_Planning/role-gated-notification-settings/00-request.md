# Role-gated notification preferences (moderator/admin) — request

*Written by the user. Free form, may be three lines. Everything below is
optional prompting, not a form to fill.*

## What I want

Wire moderator/admin-only notifications (such as moderation requests and
promotion requests) into the notification preferences UI, so that moderators
and admins can opt in to Discord notifications for them.

## Why

Moderators and admins currently have no way to enable Discord notifications
for the moderator/admin-only notification types they receive (e.g. moderation
requests, promotion requests).

## Constraints or ideas I already have

Those settings should only be visible to users with the relevant role
(moderator, admin, tech admin). This implies the notification type
registration mechanism needs to support filtering by permission/role, not
just be a flat list shown to everyone.
