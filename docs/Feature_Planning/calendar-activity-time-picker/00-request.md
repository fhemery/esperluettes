# Calendar: dedicated time field for activity dates — request

*Written by the user. Free form, may be three lines. Everything below is
optional prompting, not a form to fill.*

## What I want

In the Calendar module, when creating an event (activity), the date and time
fields are a bit painful to use. The date can be picked from the calendar, but
the time cannot. It may be worth splitting the time into its own dedicated
field.

Original wording: « Dans le module Calendar, quand on crée un événement, les

splitter l'heure en un champ dédié. »

## Why

Entering the time of an activity is tedious: the calendar picker only helps
with the date.
